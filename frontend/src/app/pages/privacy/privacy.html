1. Introduction

MediVault is a medical record management platform that helps users store and organize healthcare documents digitally. This policy explains what we collect and how we protect it.

2. What We Collect & Why

We collect account details (name, email), patient information you enter, and medical documents you upload. This information is used solely to provide your dashboard, organize your records, and maintain secure login sessions.

3. Security & Storage

Access is protected with JWT-based authentication and user-specific data isolation, so only you can see your records. Uploaded files are stored securely via Cloudinary, with access restricted by ownership validation.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data for marketing or advertising. We use trusted providers Cloudinary (file storage), MongoDB Atlas (database hosting), and Resend (email delivery) to run the platform.

5. Your Rights

You can delete your patients and records at any time, and stop using the platform whenever you choose.

6. Disclaimer

MediVault is an educational demonstration project, not a certified healthcare system. It does not provide medical advice or professional services.
